# earcut.flatten for GeoJSON

Use earcut.flatten to convert multi-dimensional arrays (like GeoJSON) into the required flat format.

Intent: To simplify data preparation when working with standard GeoJSON geometry structures.

Source: `node_modules/earcut/README.md`

Modality: recommended

> If your input is a multi-dimensional array (e.g. GeoJSON Polygon), you can convert it to the format expected by Earcut with earcut.flatten
